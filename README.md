## How I work

- Start with the business question and the decision the work needs to support.
- Check the records before relying on a dashboard.
- Automate repeatable steps and leave uncertain cases for review.
- Explain what the results show, and what they do not establish.

**Analysis and reporting:** SQL, Power BI, DAX, Excel, Tableau, Python.  
**Workflow and delivery:** Power Automate, Zoho Projects, ClickUp.
